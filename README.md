# A-Smart-Riverbank-Monitoring-System-to-Detect-An-Unauthorised-Sand-Removal
The Smart Riverbank Monitoring System detects unauthorized sand removal activities using advanced sensors and monitoring technology. It helps prevent illegal sand mining, protect riverbanks, and maintain environmental safety.
**1. Project Overview**
The Smart Riverbank Monitoring System is designed to monitor riverbank activities and detect unauthorized sand removal using sensor-based technology.
**2. Problem Statement**
Illegal sand mining causes riverbank erosion, environmental damage, and depletion of natural resources. Manual monitoring is difficult and time-consuming.
**3. Proposed System**
The proposed system uses sensors and a microcontroller to monitor riverbank conditions and identify suspicious sand removal activities.
**4. Objectives**
- To detect unauthorized sand removal.
- To monitor riverbank conditions continuously.
- To reduce illegal sand mining activities.
- To protect rivers and the surrounding environment.
**5. Applications**
- Riverbank monitoring.
- Prevention of illegal sand mining.
- Environmental protection.
- Natural resource management.
**6. Expected Outcome**
The system helps identify suspicious activities, supports effective riverbank monitoring, and promotes sustainable management of river resources.
